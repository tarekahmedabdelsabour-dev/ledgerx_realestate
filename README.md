# LedgerX Real Estate

Real estate development in one module.

- **Projects and units:** projects with their own cost center, phases, buildings, unit types, units, price lists per m², premiums.
- **Sales:** reservation with deposit, contract with a payment plan (down payment, installments, yearly extras, delivery payment, maintenance deposit), one invoice with the schedule, receipts, post-dated cheques, monthly late fees, cancellation with penalty.
- **Brokers and commissions:** due when the customer paid a set share; paid through purchase invoice or payroll.
- **Construction:** bill of quantities, contractors, advances, progress claims with retention and advance recovery, progress per building.
- **After sale:** handover with checklist and warranty, maintenance requests, owners association charges, rentals.
- **Reports:** Unit Inventory, Contracts and Collection, Installments Due, Collection Forecast, Project Profitability, Contractor Claims; dashboard cards and charts.

See `docs/INSTALL.md`. Requires erpnext and hrms.
